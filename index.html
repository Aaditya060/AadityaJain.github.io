InternKhojo — Founder & Full-Stack Developer

The internship gap for Indian students is real. I decided to close it.

Indian college students drown in scattered, untrusted internship listings. I identified the problem from my own campus, shaped the concept, designed and built the platform, defined positioning and branding, shipped it live, and drove GTM — then grew a team around it.

01 · Problem

Signal from campus

Students couldn't find trustworthy, relevant internships. Listings were fragmented across groups and sites with no student-first filter.

02 · Insight

Trust + relevance win

A student-first platform — curated listings, clear positioning, simple UX — would beat generic job boards for this audience.

03 · Product

Decisions before code

Scoped an MVP: discover internships fast, apply with confidence. Chose Next.js + Supabase for speed, auth, and iteration velocity as a solo builder.
